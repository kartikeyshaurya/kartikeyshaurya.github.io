Principles that keep showing up Across these projects

The model advises. Code decides.
Where money, credit or identity is at stake, an LLM gives an opinion and a deterministic, testable rule makes the call.
People approve risky actions.
Writes to customer systems go through review queues, approval gates or two-person rules.
Queues over hope.
Slow or flaky work runs in background workers with retries, so the user-facing path stays fast.
Degrade, don't break.
Circuit breakers, caches and switches let one failing provider slow a system down instead of stopping it.
Spend compute where it's uncertain.
CPU models handle the common case; the expensive model sees only what the cheap one can't settle.
